The John Ankerberg Show’s outreach extends far beyond the borders of America and is not limited to the weekly television program.

The combination of our broadcast and online presence has allowed us to reach more people with the Gospel and much needed discipleship materials in exciting new ways. Some of our greatest recent impact has taken place in Southeast Asia, where we broadcast in English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil to over 80% of all television homes. Combined with online and mobile initiatives, we are seeing many people around the world come to faith in Christ and grow in Him. Our approach is to use these 8 areas to connect people to God’s Word.
