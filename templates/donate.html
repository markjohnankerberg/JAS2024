Defend the faith, encourage the believer, and challenge the skeptic.

It is a time of great confusion. People are asking real questions that if left unanswered, will cause them to reject the Christian faith altogether.

At the Ankerberg Theological Research Institute (ATRI), we address tough theological and practical questions through wide-ranging forms of media (including The John Ankerberg Show) and compelling educational resources. To answer today’s pressing questions, equip believers with the best evangelical scholarship, and share the gospel worldwide, we need your support.
